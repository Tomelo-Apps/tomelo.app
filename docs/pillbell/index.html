For every dose you don’t want to miss

One pill a day, or ten at different times. If it matters that you take it, the reminder has to ring.

For you

You set it up yourself in a few steps. Any schedule, a photo of each pack, a supply warning, and a PDF list for your next doctor's visit.

For someone you look after

A parent, a partner, a grandparent, a friend. You set up their phone once. They see one big card and one big button: “I took it”. On the reliability screen, you can check that every reminder rang.

How it works

  1. Add the medicines

    Name, dose and schedule, with a photo of the pack if you like. Daily, chosen weekdays, cycles, weekly, monthly, half doses.

  2. Set up the phone once

    PillBell shows the one battery setting your brand of phone needs, then rings a test alarm so you can see it work.

  3. It rings until someone answers

    Full screen, through Do Not Disturb. Tap Taken, snooze for 15 minutes, or skip. Every alarm is recorded.

A reminder is only useful if it rings.

Many phones quietly put reminder apps to sleep to save battery, and the reminder never appears. PillBell is built to prevent that:

  • Alarm-clock priority, full screen, even on a locked phone
  • Keeps ringing until someone answers, and gets through Do Not Disturb
  • Step-by-step battery setup for Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus and other brands
  • A test alarm during setup, so you can see it work before you rely on it

See for yourself that it works.

PillBell records every alarm and shows how many rang on time over the last 30 days, for example “137 of 138”. If something on the phone gets in the way, you'll see it, and the guide shows you how to fix it.

Simple enough for anyone.

Simple mode turns the whole app into one big card: what to take next, and one large green button saying “I took it”. A photo of each pack shows which pill is which.

More when you need it.

  • Several people in one app: you, your mum, your partner
  • Any schedule: daily, chosen weekdays, every few days, 21-on/7-off cycles, weekly, monthly, half doses
  • As-needed medicines logged with one tap
  • Supply tracking with a warning before you run out
  • A PDF medicine list to take to the doctor
  • Home-screen widgets, and a lock for the app

Private in a way you can check.

PillBell doesn't have permission to use the internet at all. It isn't just a promise not to send your data: the app can't send anything anywhere, and you can see that in your phone's app details. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Backups are files you create and keep wherever you like.

What PillBell doesn't do

Better to hear it from us before you install.

  • Android only. There is no iPhone version.
  • No live alerts to family. Nothing leaves the phone, so PillBell can't message you when someone misses a dose.
  • No online sync. To move to a new phone, you use a backup file.
  • No medical advice. It is a reminder and a personal diary, not a medical device. It doesn't calculate doses or check interactions.

Questions

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones. Setup includes a step for your exact phone brand, because Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus and others each hide the battery setting in a different place.

How much does it cost?

You pay once through Google Play. There's no subscription and nothing to renew.

Can I set it up for my mum, dad or partner?

Yes. Install it on their phone, add their medicines and do the battery step there. Turn on simple mode if they find phones hard. Several people can also share one phone, each with their own profile.

Does it ring when Do Not Disturb is on?

Yes, if you allow Do Not Disturb access during setup. The guide explains each permission and why it is needed.

A reminder didn't ring. What now?

Open Settings → Reliability and the battery guide in the app. Most often the phone's battery saver silenced the app, and the guide shows how to fix it. Also make sure the app was never force-stopped. More in the guide.

What if the phone is lost or replaced?

Because nothing is stored online, make a backup file in Settings → Backup and keep it somewhere safe, such as in your email or a cloud drive. On a new phone, install PillBell, restore the file, and every reminder is set up again. PillBell reminds you to back up about once a month.

Which languages?

English, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Portuguese and Ukrainian.

Does it give medical advice?

No. PillBell is a reminder and a personal diary. It is not a medical device, does not calculate doses and does not check drug interactions. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

How do I get help?

Write to support@tomelo.app. A person reads every message. Payments and refunds go through Google Play.
