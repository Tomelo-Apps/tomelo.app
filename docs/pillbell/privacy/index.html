Privacy Policy — PillBell

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Short version: PillBell collects nothing, sends nothing and has no accounts. All your data stays on your phone. The app does not even hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything anywhere — you can verify this yourself in the app details on your phone.

Who we are

PillBell is developed and published by Tomáš Hrbatík, trading as Tomelo Apps, Janovská 401, 109 00 Praha 10 – Horní Měcholupy, Czech Republic, ID No. (IČO) 29957672. Contact: support@tomelo.app.

What data the app stores

You enter this information yourself and it is stored only in the app storage on your device:

  • medicine names, strengths, active substances, forms and your own notes,
  • reminder schedules and times,
  • a record of doses you marked as taken or skipped,
  • optional supply counts,
  • optional photos of your medicines, taken or chosen by you,
  • the names of the people (profiles) you create,
  • a technical log of whether each reminder was delivered on time.

What we do with it

Nothing beyond showing it back to you and ringing your reminders. Specifically, we do not:

  • collect, transmit, sell or share any data,
  • use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services,
  • require an account, e-mail address or sign-in,
  • store anything on our servers — we operate none for this app.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Notifications — to show your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders — so a reminder rings at the exact time, even when the phone is idle.
  • Full-screen notifications — so a reminder can be seen on a locked screen.
  • Do Not Disturb access — so a medicine reminder is not silenced.
  • Vibration and run at startup — so reminders survive a restart.
  • Camera (optional) — only when you choose to take a photo of a medicine. The photo is saved inside the app and never leaves the phone.

The app does not request internet or network access, location, contacts, microphone or file-system access.

Backups and export — you are in control

You can export your data yourself as a JSON backup, a PDF medicine list or a CSV history file. Where such a file goes is entirely your decision — you pick the destination in your phone (a folder, an e-mail, a cloud app of your choice). We never see it. If you send a file to a third-party service, that service’s own privacy policy applies to it.

Children

PillBell is not directed at children and does not knowingly process any data of children under 13 (or 16 in the EU, where applicable).

Your rights

Because we do not collect or receive your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase. All of it lives on your device: you can edit or delete individual entries in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of it permanently. Under the GDPR you retain your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection; to exercise any of them, contact us at the address above.

Not a medical device

PillBell is a reminder and a personal diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It does not calculate doses, does not check drug interactions and gives no medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date. Since the app collects no data, no change can retroactively affect information already gathered — there is none.
