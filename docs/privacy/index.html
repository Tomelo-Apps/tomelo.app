Website privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Short version: this website sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking tools. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. Each app has its own privacy policy; for PillBell, see the PillBell privacy policy.

Who is responsible

Tomáš Hrbatík, trading as Tomelo Apps. Contact: support@tomelo.app. Full details are in the legal notice.

What happens when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver pages and protect the service, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in server logs. We don't receive or use these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Visitor counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they read, which site sent them and roughly where they are (country) and what device they use. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and doesn't build a profile of you or follow you to other websites. Cloudflare doesn't keep your IP address for this. We only see totals. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is useful (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). If you block the script, the site works the same.

When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to help you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or taking steps you asked for before a purchase (Article 6(1)(b)). We keep the conversation for up to 24 months after our last message, and delete it sooner when you ask. We never add you to any mailing list unless you ask us to.

Services that handle data for us

  • GitHub (USA) hosts this website and keeps technical server logs.
  • Cloudflare (USA) runs the domain's DNS, forwards email sent to @tomelo.app addresses, and provides the cookie-free visitor counts.
  • Google (Ireland/USA) provides the Gmail mailbox where those emails arrive.

These companies may process data in the USA. They are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Links to Google Play

Our “Get it on Google Play” links include a label showing which page you came from (for example utm_campaign=home). Google Play uses it to show us how many visitors arrived from each page, as totals only. Google's privacy policy applies on Google Play.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete anything we hold about you (in practice, only emails you've sent us), and you can complain to the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).
