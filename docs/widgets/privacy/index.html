Tomelo Widgets privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Short version: Tomelo Widgets has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your calendar events, your steps and your settings stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves the phone is an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, or the city you chose. It goes to MET Norway, the Norwegian weather service, to get the weather forecast. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Who we are

Tomelo Widgets is developed and published by Tomáš Hrbatík, trading as Tomelo Apps, Janovská 401, 109 00 Praha 10 – Horní Měcholupy, Czech Republic, ID No. (IČO) 29957672. Contact: support@tomelo.app.

What data the app stores on your phone

The following is stored only in the app storage on your device:

  • your settings: language, country, formats, colours and how the widgets look,
  • the places you chose for the weather, the sky and the world clocks,
  • the latest weather forecast, so the widgets can show it,
  • your daily step goal and your step counts, if you use the steps widgets.

Calendar events are read from your phone's calendar when a calendar widget needs them. The app only reads them. It does not change your calendar, and it does not copy your events anywhere outside the phone.

What leaves the phone

Only one thing: a request for the weather forecast. To get it, the app sends the coordinates of the place to MET Norway (api.met.no) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places, which is about 1 km. If you allowed location, it is your approximate location. If you chose a city, it is that city.

The request also carries the app's name and version and our support address (TomeloWidgets/<version> (app.tomelo.widgets; support@tomelo.app)), because MET Norway asks every app to identify itself this way. It contains nothing about you.

We do not receive this request and we do not run any server for this app. MET Norway handles the request on its own servers, and its own privacy terms apply there. The forecast is used only to show the weather in your widgets.

What we do not do

Specifically, we do not:

  • collect, sell or share any data,
  • use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services,
  • use an advertising ID,
  • require an account, e-mail address or sign-in,
  • store anything on our servers. We have none for this app.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Internet (INTERNET): only to download the weather forecast from MET Norway.
  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, optional): so the weather and the sun times match where you are. You can choose cities instead and never allow location.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR, optional, read only): to show your events in the calendar widgets. Events stay on the phone.
  • Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION, optional): to count your steps with the phone's own sensor. Steps stay on the phone.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so the widgets keep updating after the phone restarts.

If you don't allow an optional permission, the app still works. Only the widget that needs it shows less.

Children

Tomelo Widgets is not directed at children and does not knowingly process any data of children under 13 (or 16 in the EU, where applicable).

Your rights

Because we do not collect or receive your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase. Everything the app stores lives on your device: you can change it in the app, and uninstalling the app removes it permanently. You can also withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's settings. Under the GDPR you retain your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection; to exercise any of them, contact us at the address above.
