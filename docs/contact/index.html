Contact

We are a small team in Prague. A person reads every message, and we usually reply within two working days, in English or Czech. Write in your own language if that's easier. We'll do our best.

Help with an app

Something doesn't work, or you have a question. It helps to mention your phone model, your Android version and which app you mean. You don't need to tell us which medicines you take. Journalists, reviewers and anyone who wants to hear about new apps: same address.

support@tomelo.app

Payments and refunds

Purchases go through Google Play. Refunds are handled by Google on Google Play's refund page.

Your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to support@tomelo.app. In practice we only hold the emails you sent us; our apps keep everything on your phone. Details are in the website privacy notice.
