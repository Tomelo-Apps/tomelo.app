What every Tomelo app promises

These apply to every app we make, now and later.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads. Where an app doesn't need the internet, it doesn't even have permission to use it, and you can check that in your phone's settings.

It works when it matters

We build for real phones, including the ones that put apps to sleep to save battery, and we walk you through the one setting your phone needs.

Simple enough for anyone

Big buttons, clear words, and a simple mode for people who find phones hard. Easy for everyone, including older people and the families who help them.

Pay once. Never a subscription.

One payment, no renewals to keep track of. We earn money from apps worth buying, not from your data.
