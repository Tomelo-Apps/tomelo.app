What every Tomelo app promises

These apply to every app we make, now and later.

Your data stays yours

No account, no tracking, no ads. An app connects to the internet only when its job needs it, for example to show the weather, and it never sends your data anywhere. Where an app doesn't need the internet, it doesn't even have permission to use it, and you can check that in your phone's settings.

It works when it matters

We build for real phones, including the ones that put apps to sleep to save battery, and we walk you through the one setting your phone needs.

Clear and easy to use

Readable text, plain words and settings that make sense on the first try. No clutter and no tricks. Where it helps, like in PillBell, there is a simple mode with big buttons.

Pay once. Never a subscription.

One payment, no renewals to keep track of. We earn money from apps worth buying, not from your data.
