Tomelo Fach terms of use

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Short version: the first 30 days are free. After that Tomelo Fach is a subscription through Google Play, which renews by itself until you cancel it in Google Play. Without a subscription your data stays readable and you can export it. The app helps you write invoices, but you are responsible for what they say and for your taxes. Keep backups.

Who we are

These terms apply between us and you, the business that uses the app (“you”), and the people you invite to your business.

What the app is

Tomelo Fach is an Android app for running a trade business: enquiries, quotes, jobs, invoices, receipts, payments, messages to customers and tools for the trade. It works on your phone and, if you connect it, stores the firm's data in your own Google Drive. We run no server for it.

Free trial

Every business gets 30 days free, counted from the day the business is created in the app. No card is needed. The trial is for the business, so installing the app again or on another phone does not start a new one.

Subscription

  • After the trial, the app needs a subscription: one for a business with one person, one for a business with more people. Each is available monthly or yearly.
  • The subscription is bought, charged and renewed by Google Play, under Google Play's terms. Google Play shows the price, in your currency and with any taxes, before you buy.
  • It renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions. After cancelling, it stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Only the owner's phone buys the subscription. The other phones of the business follow it.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. Your rights as a consumer under the law of your country, where they apply, are not affected.
  • If the price changes, Google Play tells you in advance under its own rules.

Without a subscription

When the trial or the subscription ends, the app does not delete anything. You can still open and read all your data, issued invoices stay valid, and backup and export keep working. You cannot create or change jobs, quotes or invoices until the subscription is active again.

Your responsibility for invoices and taxes

The app prepares invoices, receipts and deposits by the rules of the country you choose, including VAT rates and the reverse charge. We take care to get these rules right, but the app does not give tax or legal advice, and laws change. You are responsible for the content of every document you issue, for your VAT and other taxes, for any duty to register sales (for example cash register or sales reporting rules), and for keeping your records as long as the law requires. If you are unsure about a case, ask your tax adviser.

Measurements, calculators and tables in the tools are aids. Check the results against the standards and the manufacturer's instructions that apply to your work.

Your data

Your data belongs to you. It is stored on your phones and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive; we never receive it. See the privacy policy. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore lost data. Keep the app's backups and exports, and take care of the Google account that holds your business folder. The owner decides who is invited and which role they have, and is responsible for removing access when someone leaves.

You are responsible for having the right to store your customers' data and for using it lawfully. Messages to customers are sent by you, from your own WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail.

Do not use the app for anything unlawful, do not try to get around the subscription, and do not copy, resell or reverse engineer the app beyond what the law allows.

We keep improving the app, so features can change. We may change these terms; the new version will be published at this address with a new date. If you do not agree, you can cancel the subscription. The app depends on Google Play and Google Drive, which we do not control.
