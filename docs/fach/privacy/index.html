Tomelo Fach privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Short version: Tomelo Fach has no server, no ads and no tracking. We never receive your jobs, your customers, your invoices or anything else you enter. Everything is stored on your phone. If you connect Google Drive, the firm's data is also stored in a folder in your own Google Drive and shared only with the people you invite. The app can open only the files it created itself.

Who we are

Tomelo Fach is developed and published by Tomáš Hrbatík, trading as Tomelo Apps, Janovská 401, 109 00 Praha 10 – Horní Měcholupy, Czech Republic, ID No. (IČO) 29957672. Contact: support@tomelo.app.

Your data and your customers' data

Tomelo Fach is a tool for running a trade business. You decide what goes into it: your business details, your customers' names, addresses, phone numbers and e-mails, jobs, photos, quotes, invoices and payments. For your customers' personal data, you are the controller under the GDPR. We do not receive, store or process that data, so we are not your processor.

If you connect Google Drive, the data is stored by Google under the agreement between you (and each member of your team) and Google. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

What the app stores on your phone

Only in the app's storage on your device:

  • your business details, price list, settings, numbering and due dates,
  • customers, places, jobs, quotes, invoices, receipts, payments and messages you sent,
  • photos you take for a job and signatures on reports,
  • notes, measurements and reports from the tools,
  • the people in your team, their role and their Google e-mail address,
  • the speech model for dictation, if you downloaded it.

Backups and exports (for example the ZIP file for your accountant) are files that you create and save or share yourself.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Working alone, you can use the app without a Google account. A team needs it: each person signs in with their own Google account.

The app asks Google for one permission only: drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders it created itself, or that you picked for it in Google's own file picker. It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

What the app does with it:

  • On the owner's phone it creates a folder named Tomelo Fach – <your business> with subfolders and files that hold the firm's data and photos.
  • When you invite someone, it shares the folders that person's role needs with their Google e-mail address. Google sends them an e-mail about the shared folder. A technician never gets the folder with invoices and payments.
  • It reads and writes these files to keep the phones of your team in step.
  • It uses your Google e-mail address to share folders and to show who changed what.

The sign-in itself is handled by Google on your phone. We never see your password, and the app sends the data only between your phone and Google, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We do not receive it.

Tomelo Fach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to store and share your firm's data as described here. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and no person reads it except you and the people you invite.

You can stop the access at any time: remove Tomelo Fach in your Google Account under Third-party apps and services. The folder in your Google Drive stays yours: you can keep it, stop sharing it, or delete it.

Dictation

Dictation is optional. When you turn it on, the app downloads a speech model (about 670 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), where the model is published. Like any download, the request shows your IP address to Hugging Face; it carries nothing about you or your business. You can choose to download only over Wi-Fi.

Speech is turned into text on your phone, without internet. The recording is not sent anywhere. It is not kept, unless you turn on the option to keep recordings, which saves them in the app's folder on your phone so you can listen to them again.

Subscription and purchases

The subscription is sold and charged by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. The owner's phone asks Google Play whether the subscription is active and writes the result into the firm's data, so the other phones of the firm know it too. Google Play's own library may send Google technical information about purchases; Google's privacy policy applies to that.

Things the app opens for you

Some actions hand over to another app on your phone. The data goes there only when you use the action, and what happens next is up to that app:

  • Messages: WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail open with the text ready. You send it yourself.
  • Maps and calls: an address opens in your maps app, a phone number in the dialler.
  • Contacts: you can pick one contact in the phone's contact picker. The app gets only that contact and needs no permission to read your contacts.
  • Camera: photos are taken with the phone's camera app and saved for the job.
  • Printing: PDFs print through Android's print service.

What we do not do

Specifically, we do not:

  • run any server for this app or receive any of your data,
  • sell or share any data,
  • use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services,
  • use an advertising ID,
  • require an account with us.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): for Google Drive, the speech model download and Google Play. The app checks the connection so it can wait for Wi-Fi when you ask it to.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO, optional): only while you dictate.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, optional): reminders about tomorrow's jobs, overdue invoices and services. They are made on the phone.
  • Google Play purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING): for the subscription.
  • Run at startup, keep awake and foreground service (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Android's own job scheduler (WorkManager), so syncing and reminders keep working after a restart.

How long data is kept

On your phone, until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. In your Google Drive, until you or the owner delete the folder. Invoices often have to be kept for several years by law; that is your duty as a business, so keep your exports and backups.

Children

Tomelo Fach is a business app. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data of children.

Your rights

Because we do not receive your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase. You control everything in the app and in your Google Drive. If your customers ask about their data, you answer them as the controller; the app lets you change, export and delete customers. Under the GDPR you keep your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection; to exercise any of them towards us, write to the address above. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (ÚOOÚ) or to the authority in your country.
