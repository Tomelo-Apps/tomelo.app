Everything a small firm needs

For one person or a small team. Pick your trade, and the app sets up the price list, the tools and the kinds of jobs.

Quotes that become jobs

Build a quote from your price list and send it as a PDF. When the customer agrees, it becomes the job with the same items.

Invoices with the right VAT

Invoices, deposits and credit notes with the rules of your country, including the reverse charge in construction. Numbering and due dates are set once.

Paid on the spot

A quick receipt for small jobs paid in cash or by card, where your country allows it. Reminders for invoices that are overdue.

Talk instead of typing

Dictate what you did while it is fresh. The phone turns speech into text without internet, and you check it before anything is saved.

Tools for your trade

Calculators for materials and cables, a level, a ruler and a light meter. Handover and test reports the customer signs on the screen.

A team that sees its part

Owner, office and technician. A technician sees the jobs assigned to them and the prices, never your invoices and payments.

Messages to the customer

Ready texts for WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail: on my way, quote ready, invoice sent. You send them yourself, from your phone.

Export for your accountant

Invoices and payments for a month, a quarter or a year in one ZIP file: tables for Excel and every PDF.

Your data stays yours.

Tomelo Fach has no server, and we never see your jobs, customers or invoices. Everything is on your phone. If you work with a team, the firm's data is kept in your own Google Drive, in a folder the app creates, and shared only with the people you invite. The app can open only the files it made itself.

What Tomelo Fach doesn't do

Better to hear it from us before you install.

  • Android only. There is no iPhone version and no computer version yet.
  • Not accounting software. It issues invoices and receipts and exports them for your accountant. Bookkeeping and tax returns stay with your accountant.
  • Not tax advice. The VAT rules follow the law of each country. If you are unsure about a case, ask your tax adviser.
  • Six countries. Czechia, Slovakia, Germany, Austria, the United Kingdom and Ireland.
  • A team needs Google accounts. Everyone signs in with their own Google account. On your own you can work without one.

Questions

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything works offline. Changes reach the others the next time the phone is online.

Where is my data?

On your phone. With a team, also in a folder in the owner's Google Drive. We have no server and never receive your data.

What if I lose my phone?

If your Google Drive is connected, install the app on the new phone and choose that you are the owner and already have the business on another device. Working alone without Google Drive, make a backup in the settings now and then and keep the file safe.

How do I add my team?

In Settings, open People, add a person with their Google e-mail and send the invite. They install the app, choose “Join an existing business” and paste the link from the message.

How much does it cost?

The first 30 days are free, without a card. After that it is a subscription through Google Play, monthly or yearly, and Google Play shows the price in your currency. Without a subscription you can still read your data, issued invoices stay valid, and backup and export keep working.

When can I get it?

Tomelo Fach is not on Google Play yet. It will be there soon.

How do I get help?

Write to support@tomelo.app. A person reads every message.
